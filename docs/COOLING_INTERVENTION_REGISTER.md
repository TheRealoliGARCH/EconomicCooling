# Cooling intervention register — initial evidence build

This register is an evidence inventory, not yet a treatment dataset. It records reported district-cooling project milestones to guide primary-source verification and spatial matching.

## Interpretation rules

- A year in a contractor portfolio is recorded as a reported project/track-record year, not silently re-labelled as a commissioning year.
- Capacity is retained in refrigeration tons (RT) as reported. Do not aggregate capacities across overlapping service areas or treat installed/production capacity as connected or utilised capacity.
- `provisional` means the project is a candidate for further verification, not an admissible causal treatment date.
- `hold` means a material source conflict must be resolved before use.
- A project can enter the causal treatment panel only after confirming (i) actual operational/commissioning date, (ii) service boundary, (iii) capacity or connections actually in service, and (iv) a defensible comparison area.
- This file does not establish neighbourhood economic outcomes, cooling delivered, utilisation, or causal effects.

## Initial evidence

The initial register includes project milestones reported by ADC Energy Systems across Dubai, Doha, Abu Dhabi and Riyadh, plus operational milestones reported by Empower and Dubai Government Media Office. ADC's project page is a contractor portfolio and therefore secondary project evidence. Empower's integrated report and government media-office releases provide stronger company-reported operational milestones, but still require independent verification and geospatial service boundaries.

The West Bay DCP 3 entry is held because the contractor page's track-record table reports 35,000 RT while its project description reports 40,000 RT. This discrepancy must not be resolved by choosing one value without further evidence.

1. Retrieve original owner/operator annual reports and project handover/commissioning announcements.
2. Obtain plant-level commissioning month, phase capacity, connected load, annual cooling sales and buildings served.
3. Geocode network/service boundaries and identify untreated or not-yet-treated neighbourhoods.
4. Build a project-neighbourhood-year exposure measure; do not assign a whole-city binary treatment.
5. Record source page/table, publication date, retrieval date and exact date precision for every verified field.
